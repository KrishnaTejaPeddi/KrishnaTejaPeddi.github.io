he was alive during  the han dynasty, and the main reason he was so focused on preserving china's history actually was because his father, sima tan had started a huge
project before he died, and  he asked sima qian to finish it, making this book called "shiji" wasnt just for history, but also a way for him to honour his dads legacy. his
book "shiji" became extremely influential,the shiji mixed  genealogies, essays, biographies, and  chronological records which became the standard format that later chinese 
dynasty history books used for nearly 2000 years. the shijin also did not limit itself to focusing on emperors and also discussed merchants, assassins, wandering knights,
scholars, etc. sima qian was very dedicated to perserving history, not just the emperors. His was formatted with Basic Annals, histories of emperors and major rulers, 
Chrological tables, timelines to help keep track of when each event happeend, treatise which were essays on topics like economics, rituals, calendars and music, heridetary
houses, the history of noble families and states and biographies, stories of individual people. the biographies tend to be the part most people talk about as its where
most of the memorable charechters appear. some of the most famous biographies of people in the shiji are assassins, like a famous example is Jing Ke, an assasin who wanted
to kill Qin Shi huang(the leader who unified china when it was disjointed, buried with the terracota army) at the time  Qin Shi Huang was known as the King of Qin and was
conquering its rival states one by one. one of these states was the state of yan. Jing Ke was sent by Prince Dan of Yan on what was basically a suicide mission, the idea was
simple, kill the king of qin, qin falls into chaos, this gives the state of yan time to survive, and Jink Ke knew that he would likely die in this process. According to Sima
Qian's account, the plan went like this: Jing Ke entered Qin's court pretending to give gifts, one gift was a map of yan territory, inside this rolled up map was a dagger, 
when the map was opened Jing Ke grabbed the dagger and attacked. Unfortunately for him the Qin broke free and fled, after a chase around in the palace, the king drew his 
sword and fought Jink ke, he won and Jink Ke died. While Jing Ke died Sima Qian admired the ambition. this assasination attempt happened around 80 years before sima qian 
born, he learned about it through official state records, court documents, earlier written history texts and oral accounts. another famous biography is the biography of 
confucius, sima qian wrote a major biography on confucius which helped preserve what much of later generations knew of him, or for example Fan li, a statesmen who helped 
defeat a rival kingdom then retired and became a wealthy merchant, sima qian used him as an example of intelligence and adapatibility,this was fairly radical for his time
as many historians did notconsider merchants worth mentioning in historical texts
