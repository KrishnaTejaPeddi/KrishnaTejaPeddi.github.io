Today is July 9th, 2026, at 9:28 PM. It is summer, and I just finished grade 11. That was fun! I also just learned about some cool clouds—specifically, many notable ones. I really like cumulonimbus clouds, lenticular clouds, and noctilucent clouds. Cumulonimbus clouds are breathtaking in size and tower up with an anvil shape on top. Lenticular clouds form a cool shape on top of mountains, and noctilucent clouds are extraordinarily high in the sky, located in the mesosphere around 76 to 85 kilometres above the Earth's surface.
